AI IN OUR STUDIO

Useful tools.
Thoughtful people behind them.

AI helps us explore, draft and build. Our people guide every step, review what comes back and take responsibility for the work we deliver.

Your brief sets the direction. Our team holds the standard.

We choose tools to suit the task, rather than fitting your project around a tool. A generated draft is a starting point: we edit, check and refine it with your goals, language and brand in mind.

HUMAN REVIEW AT EVERY STAGE

Quality is a process.
We stay involved throughout.

01

People define the brief

We agree the purpose, audience, message, references and deliverables with you before generating options.

02

People direct the tools

We prepare instructions, choose useful approaches and review each round. We discard outputs that do not fit.

03

People edit and check

We check facts and sources, brand consistency, visual errors, audio clarity and natural Hindi and English. For websites and workflows, we test the agreed interactions.

04

People approve the handover

Our team reviews the finished work and shares it for the agreed client approval. Publishing or delivery follows that review; an AI draft does not publish itself.

CARE BEYOND THE FIRST DRAFT

Your trust matters
in how we make the work.

We agree what project information may be used with AI tools and avoid putting unnecessary personal or confidential information into prompts. We check permissions for supplied assets and review the intended use of generated material before delivery.

If synthetic visuals or audio could be mistaken for real events or statements, we discuss clear labelling. We do not present generated scenes as proof of real work or a real person’s endorsement.

Curious about what this means for your project?

We would be happy to explain which steps benefit from AI, where hands-on work is needed and how we will review the result together.
